# Demo files

Books, photos and music for phone-ui away from a phone: the browser demo on the
documentation's front page, the simulator (`just sim`) and Gallery's screenshots in the
documentation.
Each folder is what the phone keeps in `/var/lib/<folder>`.

Only files anyone may share go here, with their source and license below.

## books

From [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org), public domain in the USA (and where the
author died more than 70 years ago):

- `alice-in-wonderland.epub`: Lewis Carroll, *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*, illustrated
  ([ebook 11](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11))
- `moby-dick.epub`: Herman Melville, *Moby Dick; Or, The Whale*
  ([ebook 2701](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2701))
- `the-great-gatsby.epub`: F. Scott Fitzgerald, *The Great Gatsby*
  ([ebook 64317](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/64317))

## photos

NASA's, public domain (works of the US government), from
[images.nasa.gov](https://images.nasa.gov), scaled down to 1440 pixels:

- `earthrise.jpg`: Apollo 8, Earth over the Moon's horizon (AS08-14-2383)
- `aldrin-on-the-moon.jpg`: Apollo 11, Buzz Aldrin on the Moon (AS11-40-5903)
- `blue-marble.jpg`: Apollo 17, the Earth on the way to the Moon (AS17-148-22727)
- `galaxy-cluster.jpg`: Hubble, a galaxy cluster (GSFC_20171208_Archive_e000056)
- `desert-from-the-space-station.jpg`: the International Space Station over a desert
  (ISS040-E-081008)
- `milky-way-center.jpg`: the centre of the Milky Way, from the Great Observatories (PIA12348)
