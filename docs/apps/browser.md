# Browser

Browser opens web pages: text, links, pictures and the pages' own layout and colors. It is
made for reading and is fast on the phone, but it does not run JavaScript, so sites that
need it (most web apps, logins, shops) do not work. The phone needs internet (Wi-Fi or mobile
data).

## The start page

Open **Browser** from the menu. It starts on **Start**, a list of sites that work well
without JavaScript: **Wikipedia**, **Hacker News**, **CNN Lite**, **NPR Text**, **Lobsters**
and **DuckDuckGo Lite**. Tap one to open it.

![The start page](../images/generated/browser-start.png){ width="300" }

The bar at the bottom has, from left to right, the back arrow, the address field and the
reload button.

## Opening a page or searching

1. Tap the **Address or search** field at the bottom.
2. Type an address, such as `en.m.wikipedia.org`, or words to search for.
3. Press Enter on the keyboard.

An address without spaces and with a dot in it opens as a secure (`https://`) page; you can
also type a full address starting with `http://` or `https://`. Anything else is searched
with [DuckDuckGo Lite](https://lite.duckduckgo.com/lite/), whose results are links to tap.

While a page loads, the title at the top says **Loading...** and the reload button becomes a
cross: tap it to stop, and the page you were on stays. Once loaded, the page's title shows at
the top and its address in the field.

![A web page](../images/generated/browser-page.png){ width="300" }

## Reading and following links

- Swipe up and down to scroll.
- Tap a link to follow it. A link to another part of the same page scrolls there.
- The back arrow in the bottom bar goes back to the page before, where you had scrolled to.
  On the start page, it closes Browser, as the back arrow at the top left always does.
- The reload button loads the page again and keeps your place on it.

Pictures in PNG and JPEG show once they arrive; other kinds (GIF, WebP, SVG…) do not. A
link to a picture or to a plain text file shows it; other files, such as PDFs, cannot be
shown.

## When a page does not load

Browser shows **The page could not be loaded** (titled **Not loaded**) with the reason:
**No connection** without internet, or the error the site answered, such as **HTTP 404** for
a page that does not exist. Only web addresses (`http`, `https`) can be opened.

## Limitations

- No JavaScript, and forms on pages (search boxes, logins) cannot be filled in: use the
  address bar to search.
- Nothing is kept once you close Browser: no history, bookmarks or cookies, so you cannot
  stay signed in to a site.
- One page at a time, no tabs.
