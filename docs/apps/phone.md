# Phone

Phone is where you dial numbers, see your recent calls and reach your contacts. Open it from
the dock at the bottom of the home screen, or from the menu under **Essentials**.

!!! warning "Calls do not connect yet"
    The phone does not place or receive calls through the modem yet. Dialing shows the call
    screen with **Calling...**, which changes to **No SIM card** after a moment, even with a
    SIM card in. Incoming calls do not ring. Text messages do work: see
    [Messages](messages.md). So do codes to your operator, such as checking your balance:
    see [Codes to your operator](#codes-to-your-operator).

The bar at the bottom switches between **Recents**, **Contacts** and **Keypad**. Phone opens
on **Recents**, or on **Keypad** while there are no calls yet.

## Dial a number

![The keypad, with a contact's number typed](../images/generated/phone-keypad.webp){ width="300" }

1. Tap **Keypad** at the bottom.
2. Tap the digits. For **+** (international numbers), hold **0**.
3. Tap the green :fontawesome-solid-phone: call button.

When the number belongs to someone in your contacts, their name shows under it in blue. The
:fontawesome-solid-delete-left: key on the right of the call button deletes the last digit;
hold it to clear the whole number. The call button does nothing while no number is typed.

## Codes to your operator

![A code's menu, answered once](../images/generated/phone-ussd.webp){ width="300" }

Operators answer short codes that start with **\*** or **#** and end with **#**, such as
**\*100#**, to show your balance, your remaining data, or a menu of offers. The codes depend on
your operator: look on its website or on the SIM card's packaging.

1. Tap **Keypad** at the bottom.
2. Type the code, for example **\*100#**.
3. Tap the green :fontawesome-solid-phone: call button.

Instead of a call, a screen opens with the code on the right, **Sending...** under it, and the
operator's reply on the left a few seconds later. When the reply is a menu that waits for your
choice, a **Reply** field shows at the bottom: tap it, type the number of your choice on the
keypad, and tap the :fontawesome-solid-chevron-up: send button. Each answer and the next reply
are added below.

Go back with the back button at the top left when you are done: it ends the menu with your
operator. Codes are not added to **Recents**.

When there is no reply, the screen says why: **Refused by the network** (a code your operator
does not know), **Not supported by the network**, **No answer from the network** (after 30
seconds), or **No SIM card** and **No service**, as for text messages.

Your operator may also send you such a message without being asked, after a top-up for example:
it opens on the same screen.

**\*#06#** shows the phone's IMEI, the modem's serial number, without asking the operator.

## Call someone back

![Recent calls](../images/generated/phone-recents.webp){ width="300" }

**Recents** lists your calls, the newest first:

- The name of the contact, or the number when it is not in your contacts.
- Under it, what kind of call it was: **Incoming** or **Outgoing** with how long it lasted
  (**Incoming, 1:35**), **Outgoing, not connected**, or **Missed**, in red.
- When: the time for today's calls, the day of the week for the last few days, the date
  before that.
- Several calls of the same kind in a row with the same number are one row, with their count:
  **Maya Okafor (2)**.

To call back, tap the row: the call starts at once.

To see who it was, tap the round **i** button at the end of the row. It opens the contact, or a
**New contact** with the number filled in, to save it (see [Contacts](contacts.md)).

The phone keeps your latest 500 calls.

## Clear the recent calls

1. On **Recents**, tap the :fontawesome-solid-trash-can: trash button at the top right.
2. Tap **Clear recent calls** to delete them all, or **Cancel** to keep them.

The trash button only shows on **Recents**, and only while there are calls to clear.

## Call a contact

1. Tap **Contacts** at the bottom. It is the same list as the [Contacts](contacts.md) app; the
   :fontawesome-solid-plus: plus button at the top right adds someone.
2. Tap the person.
3. Tap **Call**.

To change the number before calling (to add a prefix, for example), tap the number on the
contact instead: it opens in **Keypad**, where you can edit it, then tap the
:fontawesome-solid-phone: call button.

## During a call

![The call screen](../images/generated/phone-call.webp){ width="300" }

The call screen shows who you are calling and the call's state. The **Mute**, **Keypad** and
**Speaker** buttons light up when tapped, but do nothing yet. Tap the red
:fontawesome-solid-phone: button to hang up and go back to where you were.

Every call you start is added to **Recents**; as calls do not connect yet, they show as
**Outgoing, not connected**.
