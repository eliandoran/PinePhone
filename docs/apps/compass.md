# Compass

Compass shows which way the phone points, from its magnetic sensor. It works anywhere,
without a network or GPS.

![The phone pointing north-east](../images/generated/compass-heading.webp){ width="300" }

## Find a direction

1. Open **Compass**.
2. Hold the phone flat, screen up, and point its top edge where you want to go.
3. Read the heading at the top, in degrees and as a direction (**N**, **NE**, **E**…). The
   dial turns so that the direction the phone points is under the mark above it; the red
   **N** is north.

You can also hold the phone upright, screen facing you, as when taking a photo: the
heading is then the direction the back of the phone faces. When the top edge points
straight up or down there is no heading and the screen shows **--**.

The heading is to **magnetic** north, which can differ from true north (the north of a
map) by a few degrees, more in some parts of the world.

## Calibrate

The phone has a magnetic field of its own, which throws the compass off until it is
calibrated. Until then the screen says **Not calibrated: the heading may be far off**. You
calibrate once; the phone remembers it.

1. Move away from metal, magnets and electrical appliances.
2. Tap **Calibrate**. The dial is replaced by a tray with a ball and eight circles.
3. Tilt the phone to roll the ball into the blue circle. The ball rolls to the lowest part of
   the screen: the four inner circles are reached with a corner of the phone tilted down,
   screen up; the four outer ones with the screen tilted past upright, towards the ground. Each
   circle you fill turns green with a :fontawesome-solid-check: check, and the phone vibrates
   briefly.
4. Keep going until all eight are filled (**8 of 8**), then keep turning the phone slowly
   in every direction until the bar below is full.

![Calibrating, three circles filled](../images/generated/compass-calibration.webp){ width="300" }

Calibration takes at least 8 seconds. When it is done, the phone vibrates, the dial comes
back and the hint says **Magnetic north**. Tap **Cancel** to stop without changing the
calibration.

If it does not finish, the hint says why:

- **Keep turning, round in every direction**: the phone has not been turned enough ways.
- **Disturbed: move away from metal and magnets**: the readings do not agree with each
  other, usually because of metal nearby.

If no good calibration comes after a minute, it starts over by itself.

Calibrate again if the heading looks wrong, for example after putting the phone in a case
with a magnetic clasp.

!!! note
    The compass needs the phone's motion and magnetic sensors. If one of them does not
    work, Compass shows **No accelerometer**, **No magnetometer** or **No sensor trigger**
    instead of the dial.
