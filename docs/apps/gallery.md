# Gallery

Gallery shows the photos taken with the [Camera](camera.md) and the screenshots you took
with ++power+volume-down++, newest first.

![The Gallery's grid of photos](../images/generated/gallery-grid.webp){ width="300" }

Open **Gallery** from the menu (under **Essentials**). The photos are a grid of square
thumbnails, three to a row; scroll down for older ones. A thumbnail shows the middle of the
photo, the whole photo shows once you open it. With no photos yet, the screen says
**No photos**.

## Looking at a photo

1. Tap a thumbnail. The photo opens on its own screen, **Photo**, fitted to the screen.
2. Under it are the day and time it was taken (**14 March 2026, 09:12**) and where it is in
   the list (**1 of 12**).
3. Swipe left for the next (older) photo, right for the previous (newer) one. The photo
   follows your finger; a quick flick is enough. At the first or the last photo it only
   moves a little and comes back.
4. Tap the :fontawesome-solid-chevron-left: back arrow at the top left to go back to the grid.

![A photo opened](../images/generated/gallery-photo.webp){ width="300" }

## Deleting a photo

1. Open the photo.
2. Tap the :fontawesome-solid-trash-can: trash can at the top right. A bar appears at the
   bottom.
3. Tap **Delete photo** to delete it, or **Cancel** to keep it.

The photo is deleted from the phone at once; there is no bin to get it back from. The
viewer then shows the next photo, or goes back to the grid when it was the last one.

## Good to know

- Photos are always shown upright: the viewer does not turn when you turn the phone, and a
  landscape photo shows with dark bands above and below it.
- There is no zoom, editing, sharing or albums.
- The date shown is when the photo's file was last saved on the phone.
- Gallery shows the JPEG photos in the phone's photos folder (**/var/lib/photos**, which
  you can browse in [Files](files.md)), not those in folders inside it.
