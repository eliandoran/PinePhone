# Telegram

Telegram lets you chat on [Telegram](https://telegram.org) from the phone: your chats and
groups, their messages and photos, and your profile. The phone needs internet (Wi-Fi or
mobile data).

Telegram is not part of the firmware: it is installed separately, and then shows up in the
menu under **Internet & media**.

## Signing in

Telegram needs your own API credentials before you can sign in. They are made once on a
computer, at [my.telegram.org](https://my.telegram.org) (**API development tools**), and put on
the phone along with the app. Without them, the app says **No API credentials**.

Then on the phone:

1. Open **Telegram** from the menu. It shows **Your phone number**.
2. Type your number with the country code, e.g. +40 712 345 678, and press Enter.
3. Telegram sends a code, usually as a message in Telegram on your other devices; the app
   says where it went. Type it in the **Code** field and press Enter.
4. If your account has two-step verification, type your password too.

The phone stays signed in until you log out. Accounts that still need a step the app cannot
do (creating a new account, adding an email address) show **Not supported yet**: finish that
in another Telegram app, then sign in again.

## Chats, contacts and profile

Once you are signed in, a bar at the bottom switches between :fontawesome-solid-envelope:
**Chats**, **Contacts** and :fontawesome-solid-gear: **Profile**. The one shown is in blue.
Telegram always opens on **Chats**.

**Chats** lists your chats, newest first, with the last message and how many are unread.
Archived chats are behind the **Archived chats** row at the top. Tap a chat to read it and
write in it. Someone who deleted their Telegram account shows as **Deleted Account**.

### Replying to a message

To answer a particular message, swipe it to the left until it follows your finger, then let
go. A bar above the message field shows who wrote it and how it starts, and the keyboard
opens. Type your answer and send it, or send a photo with :fontawesome-solid-image:. To reply
to nothing after all, tap :fontawesome-solid-xmark: on the bar.

A reply shows the message it answers at the top of its bubble: who wrote it (**You** for your
own) and its first line, or only the part quoted when the sender quoted part of it in another
Telegram app. **Deleted message** means it has been deleted since.

## Notifications

Once you have signed in, Telegram keeps running in the background while the phone is in use,
even after a restart. A message that arrives while you are in another app, or on the lock
screen, then shows like a text message does:

- It slides down from the top of the screen for a few seconds, with the chat's name and the
  message (in a group, who wrote it first), and the phone vibrates. Tap it to open the chat.
- It is listed on the lock screen and in the quick settings with the Telegram icon, and the
  Telegram icon in the menu shows how many messages are unread in a red badge. Tap the
  notification to open the chat. On the lock screen, this also unlocks the phone.

There is no banner while you are in that chat already, or for messages sent without sound.
Muted chats post nothing, as in other Telegram apps. A notification goes once you have read
the chat, here or in another Telegram app. **Clear all** in the quick settings removes them
from the phone; the messages stay unread in Telegram.

!!! warning "Only while the phone is awake"
    When the screen is off and the phone sleeps, Telegram sleeps too: messages that arrived
    meanwhile show up a few seconds after you wake the phone. Unlike a text message or a
    call, a Telegram message does not wake the phone.

## Contacts

**Contacts** lists your Telegram contacts by name, each with their photo and when they were
last seen: **online** in blue, **last seen** and a time or day, or only roughly (**last seen
recently**, **within a week**, **within a month**) when they hide it. It follows them while it
is shown, and loads again each time you open Telegram.

Tap a contact to open your chat with them, even if you never wrote to each other.

These are your contacts on Telegram, not the phone's [Contacts](contacts.md). Adding or
removing contacts is done in another Telegram app for now.

## Your profile

**Profile** shows your photo (or your initials), your name and phone number, then your
**Name**, **Username**, **Bio** and **Birthday**. **None** means you have no username, bio or
birthday.

To change your name, username, bio or birthday:

1. Tap its row. A screen opens with the current value.
2. Change it with the keyboard.
3. Tap the :fontawesome-solid-check: check mark at the top right, or press Enter.

The profile shows the new value once Telegram accepts it. If Telegram refuses it, the screen
stays open and says why, for example **This username is taken.** Going back without tapping
the check mark keeps the old value.

- **Name**: your first name is needed, the last name is optional.
- **Username**: 5 to 32 letters, digits or underscores, starting with a letter. People can
  find you by it and write to you without knowing your number. Clear the field to have none.
- **Bio**: a few words about yourself, on one line (70 characters, 140 with Telegram Premium).
- **Birthday**: the **Day**, **Month** and **Year** as numbers, typed on the dial pad; the year
  is optional. A date that does not exist, such as 31 April, says **This is not a date.** Empty
  the three fields to have no birthday. Who sees it is set in Telegram's privacy settings, in
  another Telegram app for now.

To change your photo, tap it, choose a photo from the [Gallery](gallery.md) and tap the
:fontawesome-solid-check: check mark. Screenshots work too.

## Logging out

1. On **Profile**, tap **Log out** at the bottom.
2. Tap the red **Log out** to confirm, or **Cancel**.

The app then asks for a phone number again. Logging out also removes your chats from the
phone, but not from Telegram.
