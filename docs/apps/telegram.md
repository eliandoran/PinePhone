# Telegram

Telegram lets you chat on [Telegram](https://telegram.org) from the phone: your chats and
groups, their messages and photos, and your profile. The phone needs internet (Wi-Fi or
mobile data).

Telegram is not part of the firmware: it is installed separately, and then shows up in the
menu under **Internet & media**.

## Signing in

Telegram needs your own API credentials before you can sign in. They are made once on a
computer, at [my.telegram.org](https://my.telegram.org) (**API development tools**), and put on
the phone along with the app. Without them, the app says **No API credentials**.

Then on the phone:

1. Open **Telegram** from the menu. It shows **Your phone number**.
2. Type your number with the country code, e.g. +40 712 345 678, and press Enter.
3. Telegram sends a code, usually as a message in Telegram on your other devices; the app
   says where it went. Type it in the **Code** field and press Enter.
4. If your account has two-step verification, type your password too.

The phone stays signed in until you log out. Accounts that still need a step the app cannot
do (creating a new account, adding an email address) show **Not supported yet**: finish that
in another Telegram app, then sign in again.

## Chats and profile

Once you are signed in, a bar at the bottom switches between :fontawesome-solid-envelope:
**Chats** and **Profile**. The one shown is in blue. Telegram always opens on **Chats**.

**Chats** lists your chats, newest first, with the last message and how many are unread.
Archived chats are behind the **Archived chats** row at the top. Tap a chat to read it and
write in it.

## Your profile

**Profile** shows your photo (or your initials), your name and phone number, then your
**Name**, **Username** and **Bio**. **None** means you have no username, or no bio.

To change your name, username or bio:

1. Tap its row. A screen opens with the current value.
2. Change it with the keyboard.
3. Tap the :fontawesome-solid-check: check mark at the top right, or press Enter.

The profile shows the new value once Telegram accepts it. If Telegram refuses it, the screen
stays open and says why, for example **This username is taken.** Going back without tapping
the check mark keeps the old value.

- **Name**: your first name is needed, the last name is optional.
- **Username**: 5 to 32 letters, digits or underscores, starting with a letter. People can
  find you by it and write to you without knowing your number. Clear the field to have none.
- **Bio**: a few words about yourself, on one line (70 characters, 140 with Telegram Premium).

To change your photo, tap it, choose a photo from the [Gallery](gallery.md) and tap the
:fontawesome-solid-check: check mark. Screenshots work too.

## Logging out

1. On **Profile**, tap **Log out** at the bottom.
2. Tap the red **Log out** to confirm, or **Cancel**.

The app then asks for a phone number again. Logging out also removes your chats from the
phone, but not from Telegram.
