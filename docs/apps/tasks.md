# Tasks

Tasks is a list of things to do, which you tick off as you do them.

![Four tasks to do, three of them with a due date, and the row to those done](../images/generated/tasks-list.webp){ width="300" }

The tasks to do come first, grouped by when they are due:

- **Overdue**, in red: their day has passed, or their time today has.
- **Today** and **Tomorrow**: a task there shows its time, if it has one, under its title.
- **Later**: the days after tomorrow, each task with its date.
- **No date**: the tasks without one, in the order you added them.

In each group, the tasks due first come first, and a day's tasks without a time come before
those with one. While no task has a due date, the list has no headings. The tasks you ticked
off are behind the **Done** row at the end, with how many there are.

## Add a task

1. Tap **New task** at the bottom. The keyboard comes up, with a row of dates above the field.
2. To give the task a due date, tap **Today** or **Tomorrow**, or **Pick…** to choose a day
   of the month. Leave **No date** for none.
3. Type the task.
4. Press Enter on the keyboard, or tap the :fontawesome-solid-plus: plus button beside the field.

![A task being typed for tomorrow](../images/generated/tasks-new.webp){ width="300" }

The keyboard stays up, so you can type the next task right away. The date you chose stays
chosen for the next tasks too, until you tap another one or leave Tasks. When you are done,
hide the keyboard with its :fontawesome-solid-keyboard: keyboard key.

## Tick off a task

Tap the circle before a task: it fills with a :fontawesome-solid-check: check mark. The **Done**
row at the end counts it at once, and the task stays where it is for a few seconds, then goes.
Ticked by mistake? Tap the circle again before it goes: the task stays.

## See and clear the tasks done

Tap **Done** at the end of the list. The tasks you ticked off are there, the most recent first,
each with when you did it (the time today, the day this week, the date before that).

![The tasks done](../images/generated/tasks-done.webp){ width="300" }

- To put a task back among those to do, tap its circle.
- To delete all the tasks done, tap the :fontawesome-solid-trash-can: bin at the top right, then
  **Delete done tasks**, or **Cancel** to keep them. The tasks to do are not touched.

## Change or delete a task

Tap a task's title to open it.

![A task with its due date and time](../images/generated/tasks-task.webp){ width="300" }

- To change its title, tap it and type. It is saved when you hide the keyboard or go back.
  A title you empty is not kept: the task keeps the one it had.
- To delete it, tap the :fontawesome-solid-trash-can: bin at the top right, then **Delete task**,
  or **Cancel** to keep it.

## Give a task a due date

1. Open the task and tap **Due**.
2. Tap **Today**, **Tomorrow** or **Next week** (its Monday), or a day of the month. The arrows,
   or swiping sideways, show the other months.

![Choosing a due date](../images/generated/tasks-due.webp){ width="300" }

To take the date away, tap **Due**, then **None**.

Once a task has a date, you can also give it a time: tap **Time**, then **09:00**, **13:00** or
**18:00**, or set any time with the :fontawesome-solid-minus: and :fontawesome-solid-plus:
buttons, by quarters of an hour, and tap **Set**.

## Reminders

A task with a date and a time reminds you at that time: the phone vibrates and a banner shows
the task at the top of the screen. The reminder stays on the lock screen and in the quick
settings until you tap it, which opens the task, or tick the task off. A task with a date but
no time does not remind you.

If the phone was off at that time, or phone-ui was restarting, the reminder comes as soon as it
is back. Each task reminds you once; give it another date or time and it reminds you again.
Setting a time that has already passed today does not remind you.

The phone wakes from sleep for a reminder: the screen lights with the reminder on the lock
screen, and if you leave it there untouched, the phone goes back to sleep after 10 seconds.

Tasks stay on the phone when it restarts and when its software is updated.
