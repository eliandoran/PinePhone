# Tasks

Tasks is a list of things to do, which you tick off as you do them.

![Four tasks to do, three of them with a due date, and two done](../images/generated/tasks-list.webp){ width="300" }

The tasks to do come first. Those with a due date are sorted by when they are due, and show
it under their title; a due date that has passed shows in red. The tasks without one follow,
in the order you added them. Below them, under **Done**, are the ones you ticked off, the
most recent first.

## Add a task

1. Tap **New task** at the bottom. The keyboard comes up, with a row of dates above the field.
2. To give the task a due date, tap **Today** or **Tomorrow**, or **Pick…** to choose a day
   of the month. Leave **No date** for none.
3. Type the task.
4. Press Enter on the keyboard, or tap the :fontawesome-solid-plus: plus button beside the field.

![A task being typed for tomorrow](../images/generated/tasks-new.webp){ width="300" }

The keyboard stays up, so you can type the next task right away. The date you chose stays
chosen for the next tasks too, until you tap another one or leave Tasks. When you are done,
hide the keyboard with its :fontawesome-solid-keyboard: keyboard key.

## Tick off a task

Tap the circle before a task: it fills with a :fontawesome-solid-check: check mark and the task
moves down to **Done**. Tap the circle again to put it back among the tasks to do.

## Change or delete a task

Tap a task's title to open it.

![A task with its due date and time](../images/generated/tasks-task.webp){ width="300" }

- To change its title, tap it and type. It is saved when you hide the keyboard or go back.
  A title you empty is not kept: the task keeps the one it had.
- To delete it, tap the :fontawesome-solid-trash-can: bin at the top right, then **Delete task**,
  or **Cancel** to keep it.

## Give a task a due date

1. Open the task and tap **Due**.
2. Tap **Today**, **Tomorrow** or **Next week** (its Monday), or a day of the month. The arrows,
   or swiping sideways, show the other months.

![Choosing a due date](../images/generated/tasks-due.webp){ width="300" }

To take the date away, tap **Due**, then **None**.

Once a task has a date, you can also give it a time: tap **Time**, then **09:00**, **13:00** or
**18:00**, or set any time with the :fontawesome-solid-minus: and :fontawesome-solid-plus:
buttons, by quarters of an hour, and tap **Set**.

!!! note
    A due date does not alert you yet: the phone does not ring or show a notification when a
    task is due. Open Tasks to see what is due.

Tasks stay on the phone when it restarts and when its software is updated.
