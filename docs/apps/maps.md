# Maps

Maps shows the world map of [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org), the map made by
its volunteers, from whole continents down to single streets and buildings. The phone needs
internet (Wi-Fi or mobile data) to download the parts of the map it has not shown before.

![The map, zoomed in on a town](../images/generated/maps-town.webp){ width="300" }

## Moving around

Open **Maps** from the menu. The first time, it shows the whole world.

- **Move the map**: drag it with a finger.
- **Zoom in or out**: pinch with two fingers, or tap the :fontawesome-solid-plus: plus and
  :fontawesome-solid-minus: minus buttons at the bottom right.
- **Zoom in on a spot**: double tap it.

While a part of the map downloads, a blurry, enlarged version of the less detailed level
stands in for it.

When you leave, Maps remembers where you were and how far you had zoomed in, and opens there
next time.

## Without internet

The parts of the map you have seen are kept on the phone, so they show again without
internet. Places you have not seen stay blank (or blurry), and **No internet connection** shows at the top of the
map; Maps tries those parts again about every 30 seconds.

## Limitations

- Maps has no search, no route planning, and does not show where you are: find places by
  moving and zooming.
- The map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, as the corner of the map says.
