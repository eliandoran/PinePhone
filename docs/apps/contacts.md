# Contacts

Contacts is your address book: a name and a mobile number for each person. Phone and
Messages show these names instead of numbers. Open it from the menu under **Essentials**;
the same list is also in [Phone](phone.md), under **Contacts** at the bottom.

## The list

![The contacts](../images/generated/contacts-list.webp){ width="300" }

Your favorites come first, under **★ Favorites**, then everyone (favorites too) in
alphabetical order, under the first letter of their name. Names that do not start with a
letter from A to Z (a number, a symbol, an accented letter) are under **#**, at the end. The
number of contacts is at the bottom of the list.

Each person has a colored circle with their initials: the first letters of their first and
last names.

## Add someone

![A new contact](../images/generated/contacts-new.webp){ width="300" }

1. Tap the :fontawesome-solid-plus: plus button at the top right. **New contact** opens with
   the keyboard.
2. Type the name in **Name**, then press Enter.
3. Type the number in **Mobile**. The keyboard is now a dial pad: digits, **+**, **\***,
   **#**, brackets, dashes and spaces.
4. Tap the :fontawesome-solid-check: check mark at the top right, or press Enter, to save.

Both are needed: when the name or the number is missing, the phone vibrates and the keyboard
opens on that field. To leave without saving, tap the :fontawesome-solid-chevron-left: back
button at the top left.

You can also save a number from your recent calls: in **Phone › Recents**, tap the **i**
button of a number that is not in your contacts.

## A contact's page

![A contact](../images/generated/contacts-detail.webp){ width="300" }

Tap someone in the list to see their page:

- **Call** calls them (see [Phone](phone.md): calls do not connect yet).
- **Message** opens your conversation with them in [Messages](messages.md).
- **Favorite** adds them to the favorites, at the top of the list; the star turns orange.
  Tap it again to take them out.
- Tapping the number puts it in the Phone's **Keypad**, to change it before calling.

## Change or delete a contact

To change the name or the number:

1. Open the contact.
2. Tap the :fontawesome-solid-pen: edit button at the top right. **Edit contact** opens.
3. Tap the field to change and type.
4. Tap the :fontawesome-solid-check: check mark at the top right to save, or the
   :fontawesome-solid-chevron-left: back button to leave it as it was.

To delete someone:

1. Open the contact.
2. Tap the :fontawesome-solid-trash-can: trash button at the top right.
3. Tap **Delete contact**, or **Cancel** to keep them.

Their conversations and calls stay, shown with the number instead of the name.

## Numbers written differently

A number matches a contact even when it is written another way: with spaces or dashes, or
with the country code or without it (**+44 7700 900456** and **07700 900456** are the same
number). So the name shows in Phone and Messages whichever way the number came.

## Limitations

- One number per person, a mobile number. There are no email addresses, photos or other
  fields.
- Contacts cannot be imported from another phone or from the SIM card yet.
