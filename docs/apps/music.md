# Music

Music plays the MP3 files on the phone, listed by album, and keeps playing while you use
other apps.

## Putting music on the phone

Music plays the MP3 files in the phone's music folder, **/var/lib/music**, and in the
folders inside it (for example one folder per artist, one per album in it). Copy them there
from a computer. With no music yet, the app says **No music** and
**Copy MP3 files to /var/lib/music**.

The app reads the folder again each time you open it. To see songs you copied while it is
open, pull the list down from its top and let go once the :fontawesome-solid-arrows-rotate:
circle turns blue. Other kinds of files (AAC, FLAC, Ogg…) are left out.

## The library

Open **Music** from the menu (under **Internet & media**).

![The library, a song playing](../images/generated/music-library.webp){ width="300" }

- Songs are grouped by album, the albums in alphabetical order, each album's songs in their
  track order. Each row shows the title, the artist and the length.
- The names come from the tags in the files. A song without a title shows its file name,
  without an artist **Unknown artist**; songs without an album come last, under
  **Unknown album**.
- The colored square of each album is the same for all its songs; the covers show on the
  **Now Playing** screen.
- The song playing has small moving bars beside its length.

## Playing a song

1. Tap a song. It starts playing and the **Now Playing** screen opens: the album's cover (or
   a record in the album's color when the file has none), the album, the title and the
   artist.
2. Tap the large :fontawesome-solid-pause: button to pause, and again to play.
3. Drag the bar under the artist to go to another point of the song; the time played is on
   its left, the time left on its right.
4. Tap the :fontawesome-solid-chevron-left: back arrow at the top left to go back to the
   library. The music goes on.

![Now Playing](../images/generated/music-now-playing.webp){ width="300" }

The buttons, from left to right:

:fontawesome-solid-shuffle: Shuffle
:   After each song, a random other song from the library. Blue when it is on.

:fontawesome-solid-backward-step: Previous
:   Back to the start of the song. In its first 3 seconds, to the previous song.

:fontawesome-solid-play: :fontawesome-solid-pause: Play / pause
:   Pause, or play again.

:fontawesome-solid-forward-step: Next
:   The next song; after the last one in the library, the first.

:fontawesome-solid-retweet: Repeat
:   When the last song of the library ends, start again from the first. Blue when it is on.
    Without it, the music stops there.

Songs follow each other in the library's order, from one album to the next. Shuffle and
repeat stay as you set them until the phone restarts.

## While you do something else

The music keeps playing when you leave the app or open other apps. In the library, a bar at the
bottom shows the song playing, with a :fontawesome-solid-play: play / pause button and a
:fontawesome-solid-forward-step: next button; tap the bar to open **Now Playing** again. The
volume keys change the volume.

With a Bluetooth speaker or headphones connected, the music plays on them; see
[Bluetooth](settings.md#bluetooth). Their play / pause, next and previous buttons work too,
from any app.

## Good to know

- Only MP3 files are played. Covers must be embedded in the files (as JPEG).
- There are no playlists, no search and no way to delete songs from the phone.
- The length shown in the list is estimated from the file; it can differ slightly from
  the one **Now Playing** shows once the song has played a little.
