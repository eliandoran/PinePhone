# Notes

Notes keeps short texts on the phone: shopping lists, ideas, things to remember. Each
note's first line is its title.

![Three notes, the newest first](../images/generated/notes-list.webp){ width="300" }

Notes are shown as cards, two side by side, the newest first (the last changed). Each card
has the title, as much of the rest of the text as fits, and when the note was last changed
at the bottom (the time for today, the weekday for the last few days, the date before that).

## Write a note

1. Tap the round :fontawesome-solid-plus: button at the bottom right. A **New note** opens with the
   keyboard.
2. Type the title on the first line, then the rest of the note.
3. Tap the :fontawesome-solid-chevron-left: back arrow at the top left.

![A new note being typed](../images/generated/notes-new.webp){ width="300" }

There is no save button: the note is saved when you go back, and also when you hide the
keyboard with its :fontawesome-solid-chevron-down: key. A note with nothing typed is not
kept.

## Read and change a note

1. Tap the note's card. It opens to read, without the keyboard.
2. To change it, tap the text where you want to type: the keyboard comes up.
3. Go back to your notes when you are done; the changes are saved.

## Delete a note

1. Open the note.
2. Tap the :fontawesome-solid-trash-can: bin at the top right.
3. Tap **Delete note** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep it.

Deleting all the text of a note also deletes the note.

Notes stay on the phone when it restarts and when its software is updated. They are plain
text: no formatting, pictures or checklists, and there is no search yet.
