# Clock

Clock shows the time here and in a few other cities, and has an alarm, a stopwatch and a
countdown timer. The bar at the bottom switches between its four pages:
:fontawesome-solid-clock: **Clock**, :fontawesome-solid-bell: **Alarm**,
:fontawesome-solid-stopwatch: **Stopwatch** and :fontawesome-solid-hourglass-half: **Timer**.

## Time and world clocks

The **Clock** page shows the time with its seconds, the date and your time zone. Below it,
**World clock** lists New York, London, Tokyo and Sydney with their time, whether it is
already tomorrow or still yesterday there, and how many hours they are ahead or behind
you. Summer time is taken into account in each city.

![The Clock page](../images/generated/clock-world.webp){ width="300" }

The list of cities is fixed: you cannot add or remove cities yet.

To change your own time zone, go to **Settings › Date & time** and tap your zone under
**Time zone**. Clock and the world clocks follow it.

## Alarm

There is one alarm. To set it:

1. Tap **Alarm**. It is set to 07:00 at first, and is **Off**.
2. Set the time with the :fontawesome-solid-minus: minus and :fontawesome-solid-plus: plus
   buttons under **Hours** and **Minutes** (hold one to change it quickly).
3. To have it ring every week on some days, tap those days under **Repeat**: the days
   chosen turn blue. With no day chosen, it rings once, at the next 07:00 (or whatever
   time you set), then turns itself off.
4. Tap **Turn on**. Under the time, Clock says when it rings next: **Rings in 7 h 20 min**,
   or **Rings on Monday** when that is more than a day away.

![The alarm, at 07:00 on weekdays](../images/generated/clock-alarm.webp){ width="300" }

Changing the time or the days of an alarm that is on takes effect at once. **Turn off**
turns it off. The alarm is kept when the phone restarts.

At its time, the alarm fills the screen with its time, over whatever shows and over the lock
screen, as a call coming in: the phone wakes if it was asleep, and vibrates every second
and a half, even when vibration (:fontawesome-solid-bell:) is off in the quick settings.
There is no sound.

- Tap **Stop**, or press **Power**, to stop it.
- Tap **Snooze** to have it ring again in 10 minutes. Clock shows **Snoozed until 07:10**;
  **Turn off** cancels it.

If you do neither, it stops vibrating after a minute and leaves a notification,
**Missed alarm**.

## Stopwatch

1. Tap **Stopwatch**.
2. Tap **Start**. The time runs in minutes, seconds and hundredths.
3. Tap **Lap** to note a lap: each lap's own time is added at the top of the list,
   the newest first.
4. Tap **Stop** to stop it. **Start** goes on from where it stopped.
5. Once it is stopped, **Lap** becomes **Reset**: tap it to clear the time and the laps.

![The stopwatch after three laps](../images/generated/clock-stopwatch.webp){ width="300" }

The stopwatch keeps running while you look at the other pages of Clock, but it is cleared
when you leave the app.

## Timer

To count down:

1. Tap **Timer**. It is set to 05:00 at first.
2. Set the time with the :fontawesome-solid-minus: minus and :fontawesome-solid-plus: plus
   buttons under **Minutes** and **Seconds** (hold one to change it quickly), or tap **1 min**,
   **3 min**, **5 min** or **10 min**. It goes up to 99:59.
3. Tap **Start**. A bar under the time shows how much is left.
4. Tap **Pause** to stop it for a while, then **Resume** to go on.

![The timer, set to 3 minutes](../images/generated/clock-timer.webp){ width="300" }

**Cancel** stops a running timer and goes back to setting it.

The timer goes on when you leave Clock or put the phone to sleep. When the time is up,
**Time's up** fills the screen, over whatever shows and over the lock screen, as a call
coming in: the phone wakes if it was asleep, and vibrates every second and a half, even when
vibration (:fontawesome-solid-bell:) is off in the quick settings. There is no sound.

- Tap **Stop**, or press **Power**, to stop it. The timer is ready again with the time you set.
- Tap **+1 min** to run it again for a minute.

If you do neither, it stops vibrating after a minute and leaves a notification, **Timer**,
**Time's up**. The timer does not survive a restart of the phone.
