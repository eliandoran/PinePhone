Calls over 4G (VoLTE) can be turned on in **Settings › Network**, for operators whose calls over 4G the modem accepts. It is off by default, calls falling back to 2G or 3G as before; it does not work on Digi (Romania) and is untested elsewhere: <https://eliandoran.github.io/PinePhone/apps/settings/#calls-over-4g-volte>.
