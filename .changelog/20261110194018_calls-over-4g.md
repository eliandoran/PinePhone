Calls go over 4G (VoLTE) where the operator takes them there, keeping 4G and mobile data during a call; elsewhere they fall back to 2G or 3G as before. It works on Vodafone (Romania); with a Digi (Romania) SIM, whose calls over 4G the modem refused, it starts off. **Settings › Network › Calls over 4G (VoLTE)** turns it on or off: <https://eliandoran.github.io/PinePhone/mobile/#calls-over-4g-volte>.
