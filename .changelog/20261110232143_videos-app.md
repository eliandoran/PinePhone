title: Videos
---

The phone could not play a video. The new **Videos** app (in the menu, under **Internet & media**) plays the videos in its folder, which a computer sees over USB file transfer as **Videos**. The phone's video engine decodes H.264, H.265 and VP8 pictures up to 1080p, so the processor does not have to. Turn the phone sideways to watch on the whole screen: <https://eliandoran.github.io/PinePhone/apps/videos/>.
