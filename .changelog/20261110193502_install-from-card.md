title: Installing on the phone from the microSD card
---

Installing on the phone's storage used to take a computer twice: Tow-Boot's installer on a card first, then the firmware written over the USB cable. Now the firmware running from a microSD card installs itself there, with what you did while trying it: contacts, messages, photos, settings and installed apps. It also installs Tow-Boot when the phone has none.

Open **Settings › Storage** and tap **Install on the phone**: <https://eliandoran.github.io/PinePhone/install/#installing-it-on-the-phone>.
